# intro pitch

## 1. professional persona

I'm an algo dev. I take problems that people feel but don't always know how to name, and I make sh*t work.

For the past three years I was doing video and image models in the medical domain, solving embryo assessment for IVF. Before that I was automatically mapping the world for blind and visually impaired people using classic CV, mainly SLAM and 3D geometry.

I also have a BSc and MSc from HUJI.

I ask meaningful questions, understand problems from multiple perspectives, and most of the time stay one step ahead, spotting trouble from a safe distance before we're drowning in it.

I'd like to fall in love with a really hard problem, build things that users would be upset to lose, and work with awesome teams.

## 2. what I can do for you

At my previous role I built and delivered models end to end:

- **Training** — including literature survey, training the modeling itself, working with an MLOps and made everything reproducible and documented.
- **Data research, visualization & fidelity** — understanding what we're feeding the model
- **Evaluation** — defining what we're actually trying to solve and finding a meaningful proxy for it
- **Product & regulation** — working closely with model consumers, making sure requirements are grounded in what the technology can do, and that outputs meet clinical standards
- **Productization** — integrating with SW engineers so things work outside the lab too

But the technical work is only half of it.

- **Communication** — I can speak and translate the language of whoever I'm working with, even if for some odd reason they don't like math.
- **Autonomy** — I plan my own week, sync on priorities, and then you don't hear from me until I'm either: done, hit a wall, or find something that should change what we believed in.
